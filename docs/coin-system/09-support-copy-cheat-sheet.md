# 09: support copy cheat sheet

For customer support agents handling coin questions. Keep it open in a second tab.

StudsTokens are earn-only. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or converted to
money. Every new account includes three starter document unlocks, one video
lecture unlock, and one mock test unlock, which expire 30 days after signup.

If you are about to improvise, do not. Use the reply blocks below, or escalate.
Strings in quote blocks come from the approved copy deck in `06-ui-ux-spec.md`
§10. Wording is unaltered. Punctuation is plain ASCII, so a dash in the deck
appears here as a comma or a full stop. Do not extend a deck string with words
of your own, and check the deck before inventing a new line.

---

## Banned words and what to say instead

Three bans are statutory. The rest are house rules. All three categories end the
same way for you: a wrong word in an email to a student creates exposure that
support cannot undo.

| Never write | Write this instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `free`, `no cost` | `earned`, `included with your account`, `starter unlock` | If any payment, subscription, auto-renewal, or commitment is on the other side, calling it free is a statutory unfair-trade act. CPA 2075 s.16(2)(c)(3), 2 to 5 years under s.40(3)(b). |
| `worth NPR X`, `NPR X of value`, `only Rs X away`, any currency figure beside a coin figure | The coin figure only: `This costs 40 StudsTokens. Your balance is 25.` | StudsTokens cannot be bought or cashed out, so a value claim is false on the product's own terms. 07 §8.1, §8.2. |
| `prize`, `award`, `win`, `winner`, `raffle`, `draw`, `baksis`, `jitauri` | `StudsTokens`, `points`, `credits`, `balance`, `unlock` | Income Tax Act 2058 defines windfall gain to include "lottery, gift, prize, baksis, award for winning" and taxes it under s.5/88A. This is a naming constraint, not a style preference. One email saying "prize" is enough to hand the tax office the word. 07 §3.1. |
| `cash out`, `redeem for money` | `StudsTokens are only used to unlock resources on StudSphere.` | StudsTokens are earn-only and never cash-outable. 07 §8.2. |
| `refundable` | `Spent StudsTokens stay spent. If an unlock did not complete, nothing was spent.` | There is no refund path at all. 04 §4.4. |
| `you will lose` | `Your starter unlocks expire on 12 November.` | Expiry is a fact with a date, not a threat with a countdown. 06 §1.4. |
| `hurry` | Delete the word. | Urgency copy is forbidden on the insufficient screen. 06 §5. |
| `limited time` (on earned StudsTokens) | Delete the phrase. Earned StudsTokens run 12 months, extended to 18 on qualifying activity. | 06 §10, 05 §4. |
| `unlock more with` | `Unlock for 40 StudsTokens` or `Browse resources you can unlock now` | It implies purchase, and StudsTokens cannot be purchased. 06 §5. |
| `You can't afford this` | `You need 22 more StudsTokens` | On the forbidden list for the insufficient screen. 06 §5. |
| `Don't miss out!` | Delete it. | On the forbidden list for the insufficient screen. 06 §5. |
| Any countdown or ticking clock | `expires in 5 days` (a static day count) | 06 §1.4, §2.4. |
| Any emoji in a system reply | Delete it. | 06 §10. |

### The three hard bans, in one line each

- **`free`.** If there is any money or commitment on the other side, do not
  write it. The starter pack is `included with your account`, not free.
- **Currency next to StudsTokens.** No equivalence, not in a tooltip, not as a "help"
  figure, not even an approximate one, and not even to a founder or a journalist.
- **Tax-trigger words.** The one agents get wrong most often, because `prize` and
  `award` feel like compliments. They are the exact phrases the Income Tax Act
  uses to define a taxable windfall gain.

### If a student asks for a rupee figure

> StudsTokens are only used to unlock resources on StudSphere. They cannot be bought
> and they cannot be converted to money, so there is no rupee figure to give you.
> The coin cost of every resource is on its card.

Do not soften this with "roughly" or "approximately". **Never give a rounded
figure, a range, or a comparison to the printed price of a book.**

## The three questions students ask

### 1. "Why do I need StudsTokens to download this?"

```
Every new StudSphere account includes 3 starter unlocks for documents,
1 for a video lecture and 1 for a mock test. Use them by {date}.

After that, resources are unlocked with StudsTokens, and you earn StudsTokens by
completing your profile, inviting a friend, and uploading a study
resource that an admin approves. There is no way to buy StudsTokens and no way
to turn them into money, so a coin only ever unlocks something here.

You can still browse the whole catalogue without signing in.
```

Internal note: depends on whether this student's allowance is still live.
Check the `allowance` block on their balance before you send. Do not describe
the change as something they lost, and do not say it used to be free. The
allowance is the acquisition hook and the coin loop is the retention mechanic
(05 §1). Framing it as a loss is the framing that ends the relationship.

### 2. "My StudsTokens expired."

This is the sensitive one. Acknowledge, state the real date, move on.

```
The expiry date is shown against each balance on your StudsTokens page, under
"StudsToken balance, by expiry".

Starter unlocks expire 30 days after your account was created. StudsTokens you
earn expire 12 months from the day they were added, and any qualifying
activity extends that to 18 months. We send reminders 30, 7 and 1 days
before, and you can turn those off on the same page with "Email me before
StudsTokens expire."

If the date on your page does not match this, send me that date and I will
have the team check it.
```

Internal note: relies on 05 §4. Expiry is a trust tax, and customers who feel
penalised disengage rather than accelerating. The frustration data is about
earned StudsTokens vanishing, so this is where trust is lost. Do not apologise, do
not say sorry for the confusion, and do not imply the system erred. If the page
date and the ledger disagree, that is an escalation, not an apology. Never offer
an extension.

### 3. "How do I get StudsTokens quickly?"

```
There are three ways to earn StudsTokens on StudSphere.

1. Complete your profile: 25 StudsTokens in total, 5 StudsTokens for each of five
   steps, added as you finish each one.

2. Invite a friend: 60 StudsTokens for each friend who completes their first
   action and verifies their phone number. Up to 10 a month. Those 60
   StudsTokens are held for 7 days after your friend qualifies, then added to
   your balance. We confirm their first completed action before releasing.
   If they don't complete one, these 60 StudsTokens are not released.

3. Upload a study resource: 80 StudsTokens, once an admin approves and publishes
   it. We usually respond within 2 working days.
```

Internal note: never promise a release earlier than the 7-day hold (05 §3.3),
and never describe held StudsTokens as spent, lost, or under review. A hold is a
pending amount with a date, not a penalty. If the student is under 18, route
does not apply, see the referral section below. Rank the routes for them if
they have a specific resource in mind: a 15-coin profile gap is a faster answer
than a 60-coin referral that takes a week. If they ask what their friend gets,
the friend's own 25 StudsTokens show in the friend's wallet, not in yours. Never
present the friend's StudsTokens as your reason to invite.

## Error code to student-facing reply

Codes and statuses are from `03-api-contract.md` §2.3 and §4. The
`INSUFFICIENT_COINS` body already carries the shortfall and the earning routes,
so relay them rather than working them out yourself.

| Code | Status | Exact reply | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| `INSUFFICIENT_COINS` | 402 | `You need {gap} more StudsTokens. This resource costs {price} StudsTokens. Your balance is {balance}. You can close the gap in three ways: {route} +{n} StudsTokens, {route} +{n} StudsTokens, {route} +{n} StudsTokens. You can also browse resources you can unlock now.` | Never a rupee figure, never `you can't afford this`, never a countdown, never `free`. The routes in the error body are already filtered to what this student is still eligible for, so a completed profile will not appear. |
| `ALREADY_OWNED` | 200, not an error | `Already unlocked, nothing was spent.` | **Never tell a student they were charged, and never tell them a charge is pending or will be reversed.** The system takes nothing on this path. This is the single most damaging wrong answer in the table. |
| `ALLOWANCE_EXPIRED` | 423 | `Your starter unlocks expired on {date}. You can still unlock this with {price} StudsTokens, and your balance is {balance}.` | No apology, no extension, no "it should not have expired". If the wallet page was showing a live allowance when they hit this, escalate. |
| `RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | `That link does not match a resource we can offer right now. Nothing was spent and your balance has not changed. You can browse resources you can unlock now and try the one you want.` | Do not guess why it is missing, and do not reuse the `ALREADY_OWNED` reply here, that would be false. |
| `INVALID_IDEMPOTENCY_KEY`, `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSE` | 400, 409 | `That unlock was not completed. Your balance has not changed. Please try once more, and if it fails again I will have engineering look at it.` | Never mention keys, retries, or duplicate requests to a student. |
| Unauthenticated, 401 | 401 | `Log in to unlock.` | Never answer a 401 as if the student had no StudsTokens. "Log in", "you have no StudsTokens" and "you already own this" are three different answers. |
| StudsToken account frozen (`ErrAccountFrozen`) | 423 | `We cannot change a StudsToken balance while an account hold is in place. Nothing was spent. I have asked the team to review the hold and come back to you.` | Never speculate about the cause of the hold, never give a release date, never suggest opening another account. |
| `CAP_REACHED` | 409 | `You've reached the monthly invite limit. It resets on {date}.` | Do not disable the framing: the student may still share, a referral next month is still a referral. |
| `SELF` (self-referral) | 400 | `We can only count a referral from a different person, once. This account cannot use its own code.` | Never accuse the student of fraud, and never explain how the check works. |
| `SUSPENDED` (referral code) | n/a | `That referral code is not active. Please register without it and use your starter unlocks.` | Never say why a code is suspended. Escalate. |
| Throttled request | no coin error code exists yet in `03` | `Too many attempts in a short time. Please wait a few minutes and try again. Nothing was spent.` | Do not quote a code name to the student, the contract does not define one for StudsTokens. Escalate if it recurs. |
| Anything unexpected, 500 | 500 | `Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was spent. I have passed this to the team.` | Never guess a cause. |

### If the student says an email never arrived

Only these events send an email, and each fires inside the same transaction as
the change that caused it, so a rolled-back change never produces one (03 §5).
A missing email is not proof the change did not happen, so check the balance
first. There is no email when a hold is placed, only when StudsTokens are released or
revoked, so a student waiting on held referral StudsTokens wants the hold date from
their referral page, not an apology about a missing email.

| Event key | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `coins.debited` | a spend went through. `coins.credited` is not implemented yet; it arrives with the earn phase |
| `referral.qualified` / `referral.released` / `referral.revoked` | A friend qualified / held StudsTokens landed / held StudsTokens were taken back |
| `allowance.expiring` / `allowance.expired` | Starter pack reminders and lapse |
| `resource.approved` / `resource.rejected` | Upload outcome |

## What support must never promise

- **No refund on spent StudsTokens.** There is no refund path. Turning a gate off does
  not refund StudsTokens already spent; a student who spent keeps the unlock (04 §4.4).
- **No cash value and no equivalent.** StudsTokens cannot be purchased, sold, or
  cashed out (D1, 07 §8.1 and §8.2). A balance is a unit of account, not money.
- **No expiry extension on request.** Earned StudsTokens extend automatically on
  qualifying activity, not on a request (05 §4). Changing expiry needs
  conspicuous notice, so it is not a support decision (07 §8.10).
- **No date for when a resource will be added.** There is no such commitment in
  the copy deck. The only approved time commitment is
  `We usually respond within 2 working days` for an upload review, and that must
  not be stretched into a promise about the catalogue.
- **No compensation for an expired balance.** Breakage on unredeemed StudsTokens is an
  open question for the accountant (07 §2.5), and no promise of a future rule
  change, such as "that might change".

### The balance trap

If a student says their balance is wrong, **do not guess and do not hand-edit.**

A balance is a ledger, not a number. Support has no tool that sets a balance.
The only fix is a recorded adjustment by an admin, with a reason code, which
appears in the student's own transaction history as its own entry (03 §3.2,
05 §3.5). A balance you cannot explain is a balance you cannot defend.

> I have asked the team to review this. Any change to a balance is recorded
> with a reason and will appear in your transaction history. I am not able to
> change it from here myself.

Then escalate. **Never say "I will add StudsTokens to your account"**, and never give
a number you expect the team to arrive at. Never ask for a password, and never
ask a student to change anything to make a balance look right.

## The 18+ referral rule, and how to answer it

Referral StudsTokens are only available on accounts that declare 18 or over. This is a
legal requirement under Nepal's Privacy Act 2075, not a product preference.

Why it exists, in one line: inviting someone shares your name and some account
details with that other person, and s.33 requires guardian consent plus a
benefit test before a minor's personal information is disclosed to another
person. We do not collect guardian consent, so the referral earn is limited to
18+ accounts (07 §5.2, §8.8). A referral is also strictly single-level: only the
person who sent the invite earns, and StudsTokens cannot be transferred between
accounts (07 §8.3, §8.14).

```
The referral reward is only available on accounts that declare 18 or over.

Inviting a friend shares your details with that friend, and for under-18s
Nepal's Privacy Act 2075 requires guardian consent before a minor's
information is shared with another person. We do not collect that consent,
so the referral earn is limited to 18+ accounts. This is a legal
requirement, not something we can change.

Everything else about StudsTokens is the same on your account: the 3 starter
unlocks included with your account, profile StudsTokens, and upload StudsTokens.
```

You will get pushback. Expect "my friend is 17 too" and "just let me invite one
person".

- **Say plainly that it is a legal requirement.** Do not present it as a rule
  that might be relaxed.
- **Do not ask for proof of age.** The gate is a self-declaration. Asking a
  student for a birth certificate or a school ID is a privacy problem of its own.
- **Never suggest a parent create or hold the account.** That moves a minor's
  data further, not less. **Never offer StudsTokens as a workaround** for not being
  able to refer. Offer the other two earn routes instead and let them choose.

## When to stop answering and escalate

Stop answering and hand over if any of these appear. Escalating costs an agent
ten minutes. Improvising costs the company far more.

**To engineering:**

- A balance that does not match the transaction history.
- StudsTokens appearing or disappearing with no matching entry. Nothing is ever edited
  or hidden, so a missing entry is a defect.
- A double spend, a double charge, or a student charged for an unlock that shows
  as unlocked. Also an unlock that failed but still moved the balance, or a
  success with no entry.
- `ALLOWANCE_EXPIRED` while the wallet page was showing a live allowance. The
  coin gates have config kill switches, so a mismatch here is a config or
  deployment fault.
- A resource 404ing for a student who can see it listed, or repeated throttling.

**To finance and the founders, not to engineering:**

- **Any press or social media attention** to expired StudsTokens, to a self-referral
  scheme, or to a coin complaint. The misleading-advertising band is the top
  penalty band at 2 to 5 years, and it carries an on-the-spot fine with no court
  required (07 §5.3). Do not respond publicly, even to correct a factual error.
- **Any request for a coin refund at volume**, or any group claim. There is no
  refund mechanism, so a volume request is a policy question.
- Any request for a rupee value, a coin-to-money conversion, or a claim that
  StudsTokens are a store of value.
- Any request to buy, sell, or transfer StudsTokens, including between two of a
  student's own accounts. Do not build the workaround and do not suggest one.
- Any mention of tax on the student's side, or of windfall gain.
- Any question about VAT, breakage, or the accountant's open questions. The VAT
  classification is answered and does not need explaining to a student; the
  written reasoning and the breakage question are still open, and a chartered
  accountant and an advocate must sign off before launch (07 §2.6 and §7).

**To engineering and legal together:**

- Any request to reproduce past papers, or a claim from an author or institution
  about copyright in question papers.
- Any implied NEB, HEB, CHE, TU, or university endorsement. Never imply it, and
  never repeat it if a student says it for us.
